## 0. Executive summary

The column skeleton (placement, lid state, heat network, meltdown sequence, DODD overlay, panels, loader) is a careful and largely faithful port — most framework-level differences are honest `[GLUE]` and the port comments correctly identify the original lines they translate. The **debris family is where fidelity breaks down**: the port consistently cites a "CE" source (the 1.18.2 community remake referenced in `PORT:client/render/item/RBMKColumnItemRenderer.java`) and imports CE numbers/behaviors that **do not exist in the authoritative 1.7.10 tree** — 10x debris lifetimes, graphite debris losing its radiation, a rebalanced radiating-debris radiation field without line-of-sight occlusion, an added fire field on burning debris, added digamma radiation, and a different suppressant block set. There are also three port-only content items (RBMK slab pair, two deco panel blocks) with no 1.7.10 analog.

**Totals: 14 [DEVIATION], 1 [ABSENT], 5 [EXTRA]** (findings are enumerated in §2; verdicts per topic in §3).

## 1. KEY QUESTIONS — explicit answers

### Q1. What is port RBMKColumnBlock (+ BE + filler + item) mapped to in the original?

**Mapping:** `RBMKColumnBlock` (abstract, `PORT:block/machines/rbmk/RBMKColumnBlock.java:33`) is the port of **`ORIG:blocks/machine/rbmk/RBMKBase.java:35`** (`RBMKBase extends BlockDummyable implements IToolable, ILookOverlay`) — i.e. the *shared base class* of all RBMK columns, not `RBMKBlank` itself. The concrete `RBMKBlankBlock` (`PORT:.../RBMKBlankBlock.java:14`) maps to `ORIG:blocks/machine/rbmk/RBMKBlank.java:8` (+ `ORIG:tileentity/machine/rbmk/TileEntityRBMKBlank.java`), and every other column type subclasses the same base exactly as upstream.

**Classification: [GLUE] for the skeleton, with faithful ported logic on top.** The decomposition:

- `RBMKColumnFillerBlock` (`PORT:block/machines/rbmk/RBMKColumnFillerBlock.java:19`) is the structural analog of the original's **dummy blocks**: in 1.7.10 `BlockDummyable.fillSpace` fills `getDimensions(world) = {h,0,0,0,0,0}` — h dummy blocks *UP* from the core (`ORIG:blocks/BlockDummyable.java:364` "order is UP, DOWN, FORWARD, BACKWARD, LEFT, RIGHT"; `ORIG:blocks/machine/rbmk/RBMKBase.java:178-180`, `fillSpace` 167-171) — each dummy being a real block with real collision. The port renders the whole column from the base BE (`RenderShape.ENTITYBLOCK_ANIMATED`, `PORT:RBMKColumnBlock.java:45-48`) and so needs invisible collision segments above it: `setPlacedBy` → `fillFillers` places `RBMKColumnFillerBlock` for `i = 1..getColumnHeight(level)` (`PORT:RBMKColumnBlock.java:194-235`), matching the h+1-block column (h = `RBMKDials.getColumnHeight` = gamerule `dialColumnHeight` clamped 2..16 minus 1 — `PORT:handler/rbmk/RBMKDials.java:64-66` 1:1 with `ORIG:tileentity/machine/rbmk/RBMKDials.java:96,207-208`, default 4 → 3). The filler's lid bump (`SHAPE_WITH_LID` = 20/16 = 1.25 tall on the topmost segment, `PORT:RBMKColumnFillerBlock.java:22,72-86`) reproduces `ORIG:RBMKBase.getCollisionBoundingBoxFromPool:127-148` (+0.25 when the core has a lid). Filler break routes to the whole column (`destroyWholeColumn`, `PORT:RBMKColumnFillerBlock.java:111-117`) like `BlockDummyable`'s dummy→core break routing (`ORIG:BlockDummyable.java:384-393`), pick-block and mining progress delegate to the base, and `RBMKColumnFillerBlock.findBase` plays the role of `BlockDummyable.findCore`.
- `RBMKColumnBlockEntity` (`PORT:blockentity/machines/rbmk/RBMKColumnBlockEntity.java:37`) maps 1:1 to `ORIG:tileentity/machine/rbmk/TileEntityRBMKBase.java:53`: same NBT keys (`heat`, `reasimWater`, `reasimSteam` — `PORT:564-597` vs `ORIG:268-289`), same heat-equalization math with neighbor cache (`PORT:122-181` vs `ORIG:159-234`), same ReaSim boiler dial behavior (`PORT:183-192` vs `ORIG:128-145`), same `maxHeat()=1500`, `passiveCooling` formula and 20-heat floor (`PORT:194-201` vs `ORIG:82-95,256-259`), same lid semantics (`hasLid()` short-circuit on `!isLidRemovable()` — `PORT:488` vs `ORIG:65-75`), and the full meltdown pipeline (§1d below). It is NOT a port-invented invention; it is the TileEntity base ported onto the BE framework.
- `RBMKColumnBlockItem` (`PORT:item/rbmk/RBMKColumnBlockItem.java:14`) is BEWLR plumbing so the item icon uses the same column renderer — `[GLUE]` (the original relied on ISBRH `renderInventoryBlock`; the port equivalent lives in `PORT:client/render/item/RBMKColumnInventoryRender.java`, which replicates the original `RenderRBMK*#renderInventoryBlock` poses).

So: the trio is **[GLUE] structure skeleton (filler = dummy-block analog) + [faithful port] (base block/BE logic)**. No [DEVIATION] in the mapping itself.

**No.** Exhaustive search of the 1.7.10 tree (`grep -rin "RBMKSlab|rbmk_panel_slab|panel_slab|slab2"` over `src/main/java` and assets) finds **nothing**; the only slabs are `BlockMultiSlab` concrete/brick/stones/pink (`ORIG:blocks/ModBlocks.java:1528-1535, 2364-2365`). `RBMKSlabBlock`/`RBMKSlabItem` (`PORT:block/generic/RBMKSlabBlock.java:26`, `PORT:block/generic/RBMKSlabItem.java:23`, registered as `deco_rbmk_panel_slab4`/`deco_rbmk_panel_slab2` at `PORT:block/ModBlocks.java:2370-2378`) are therefore **[EXTRA] relative to 1.7.10**. Provenance (read-only git): both files exist on `origin/FFA` **and** `origin/machines`; the working-tree javadoc attributes them to "CE's `BlockRBMKSlab`" — i.e. the 1.18.2 community remake, not the 1.7.10 source of truth. Under the audit's canon rules this is port-only material that needs an explicit exception justification; the two-pixel/two-height stacking design itself is internally consistent, but it is not 1.7.10 fidelity.

### Q3. RBMKBase interactions 1:1?

**Placement.** Original: `BlockDummyable.onBlockPlacedBy` → `getDirModified` forces `DIR_NO_LID` (`ORIG:RBMKBase.java:174-176`) → `fillSpace` fills the column and `makeExtra`s the top block for pipe connectors (`ORIG:RBMKBase.java:167-171`). Port: base block placed as a normal block; `setPlacedBy` fills fillers (`PORT:RBMKColumnBlock.java:194-198`); new columns have `lidState = 0` (`PORT:blockentity/.../RBMKColumnBlockEntity.java:58`, with comment citing `getDirModified`) — matching upstream "columns are always placed uncapped; lids are a separate item". A deferred `onPlace` tick re-fills fillers for engine/`/setblock` placements (`PORT:RBMKColumnBlock.java:203-218`) — `[GLUE]` recovery for the missing `MultiblockHandlerXR` space-check path. `makeExtra`'s pipe-connector meta has no analog; the port attaches pipes through the loader block and the BE fluid ports (`ORIG:TileEntityRBMKBase.getPorts*Loader`, 612-644) — `[GLUE]`.

**Right-click.** Original: only GUI-bearing columns override `onBlockActivated` → `openInv` (boiler/storage/heater/control/control-auto/outgasser, e.g. `ORIG:RBMKBoiler.java:33-36`); non-GUI columns (blank, rod, moderator, absorber, reflector, cooler) fall straight through to *item* use, so lid/tool work on them without sneaking. `openInv` itself (`ORIG:RBMKBase.java:105-125`): skip GUI when holding a lid item and the column is capped; open GUI id 0 only when not sneaking. Port: one `hbmOnUse` router (`PORT:RBMKColumnBlock.java:78-114`) — tool → screwdriver → lid → GUI-if-not-sneaking → PASS. Sneak+empty-hand = PASS on both sides. **[DEVIATION] (minor)**: the port intercepts the RBMK tool and lid items *before* the GUI check on all columns, so on GUI-bearing columns a plain right-click links the tool / places a lid where upstream consumed the click for the GUI (tool linking upstream required sneak, per 1.7.10 item-use-bypass-on-sneak). The port's own lore still says "Sneak and right-click RBMK…" (`PORT:datagen/RestportLang.java:2996`), so code and text disagree.

**Lid placement/removal.** Original: `ItemRBMKLid.onItemUse` → find core → refuse if `hasLid()` → set meta `DIR_NORMAL_LID`/`DIR_GLASS_LID` + `node.addLid()` + concrete/glass sound + shrink (`ORIG:items/machine/ItemRBMKLid.java:27-72`). Screwdriver: `RBMKBase.onScrew` (ToolType.SCREWDRIVER only) → refuse unless `hasLid() && isLidRemovable()` → `node.removeLid()` → spawn lid item at core top → reset meta to `DIR_NO_LID` (`ORIG:RBMKBase.java:199-239`). Break: `breakBlock` drops the concrete/glass lid at column top when `dropLids` (`ORIG:RBMKBase.java:183-196`; `dropLids=false` bracketed around the whole meltdown, `ORIG:TileEntityRBMKBase.java:441,568`). Port: lid item `useOn` resolves the column core via `findColumnCore` and requires `isLidRemovable() && !hasLid()` → `setLidState(1|2)` + glass/stone sound + shrink (`PORT:item/rbmk/RBMKLidItem.java:24-47`); screwdriver handled in `hbmOnUse` → `dropLid` + `setLidState(0)` (which also updates the neutron node, `PORT:blockentity/.../RBMKColumnBlockEntity.java:500-508`) (`PORT:RBMKColumnBlock.java:94-98`); `onColumnRemoved` drops lid on break gated by `dropLids`/`ContraptionAssemblyGuard` (`PORT:RBMKColumnBlock.java:259-270`). Semantics match, including glass-vs-concrete distinctions and the drop position above the column top. Small deltas: sounds are `STONE_PLACE`/`GLASS_PLACE` instead of the original's concrete_smooth/glass step sounds (`[COSMETIC]`); the port also drops the lid when the block is destroyed by **explosion** (`onRemove` fires for air replacements), where 1.7.10 explosions bypassed `breakBlock` and lost the lid — **[DEVIATION] (minor, arguably an improvement)**. The original's `explodeOnBroken` meta-swap guard (`ORIG:RBMKBase.java:228-230`) is structurally unnecessary in the port because lid state lives in BE NBT, not block metadata — `[GLUE]`.

**Bounding shapes.** Original column = full 1×(h+1)×1 blocks (dummy blocks carry their own full collision), lid bump +0.25 on the top. Port = base full block + h invisible full-block fillers + 0.25 lid bump on the topmost filler only (`PORT:RBMKColumnFillerBlock.java:72-86`), selection outline follows collision. Equivalent. One real caveat: the **renderer** uses the *static* `RBMKDials.COLUMN_HEIGHT + 1` (`PORT:client/render/implementations/RBMKColumnRenderer.java:130`) and so does `getRenderBoundingBox` (`PORT:RBMKColumnBlockEntity.java:526-530`), while fillers/melt use the *dynamic* `getColumnHeight(level)` — with a non-default `dialColumnHeight` the drawn column height stops matching the physical one. **[DEVIATION] (only under a changed gamerule).**

## 2. Findings register

### Debris entity — `PORT:entity/rbmk/RBMKDebrisEntity.java` vs `ORIG:entity/projectile/EntityRBMKDebris.java`

- **[DEVIATION] D1 — debris lifetimes ×10 across all six types.** Port: BLANK/ELEMENT 30 min, FUEL 100 min, GRAPHITE 150 min, LID 5 min, ROD 10 min (`PORT:RBMKDebrisEntity.java:204-212`). Original: BLANK/ELEMENT `3*60*20` ticks = 3 min, FUEL `10*60*20` = 10 min, GRAPHITE `15*60*20` = 15 min, LID `30*20` = 30 s, ROD `60*20` = 60 s (`ORIG:EntityRBMKDebris.java:139-150`). The port comment claims the originals "were a tenth of CE's figure" — true only for the CE remake, not for 1.7.10.
- **[DEVIATION] D2 — graphite debris no longer irradiates; fuel radiation rebalanced.** Original: FUEL **and** GRAPHITE apply the radiation potion within `boundingBox.expand(2.5)`, amplifier 9 (fuel) / 4 (graphite), 60 s (`ORIG:EntityRBMKDebris.java:100-107`). Port: FUEL only, inverse-square dose `100 / max(len, 0.8)²` over 16 blocks via `ContaminationUtil` (`PORT:RBMKDebrisEntity.java:159-184`). The comment again cites "CE … range 16, strength 100".
- **[DEVIATION] D3 — (with D1/D2) the per-type lore comments in the port systematically cite CE numbers that do not exist in the authoritative tree.** Wherever a debris parameter differs, the justification references CE, not 1.7.10.

### Debris blocks — `PORT:block/machines/rbmk/RBMKDebris*Block.java` vs `ORIG:blocks/machine/rbmk/RBMKDebris*.java`

- **[DEVIATION] D4 — burning debris gained a fire-damage field with no 1.7.10 analog.** Port `scorch()` — inverse-square inFire damage over 32 blocks + 5 s ignite, every tick cycle (`PORT:RBMKDebrisBurningBlock.java:41-43,55,92-113`). Original `RBMKDebrisBurning` damages nobody (`ORIG:RBMKDebrisBurning.java:23-65`); only flame/gas/sound/burn-out. (CE cites `radiate(.., 32, 0, 0, 50)` — no such method exists in `ORIG:util/ContaminationUtil.java`.)
- **[DEVIATION] D5 — burning debris suppressant set reduced.** Original counts `foam_layer || block_foam || sand_boron_layer || sand_mix(BORON)` for the 1-in-10 fast burn-out (`ORIG:RBMKDebrisBurning.java:37-44`). Port counts only `SAND_BORON` (`PORT:RBMKDebrisBurningBlock.java:72-86`) — and the port *does* register `FOAM_LAYER`/`BLOCK_FOAM`/`SAND_BORON_LAYER` (`PORT:block/ModBlocks.java:4241-4246`), so the omission is not forced.
- **[DEVIATION] D6 — radiating debris radiation rebalanced and occlusion removed.** Original: 1 000 000 rads over **100** blocks, dose attenuated by summing `getExplosionResistance` along the ray (walls block), `inFire 100` within 5 blocks, marshmallow roasting within 10 (`ORIG:RBMKDebrisRadiating.java:29-49,73-120`). Marshmallow roasting is preserved (1-in-100, <10 blocks, main hand) and decay 0-15 → `rbmk_debris_burning` matches (`PORT:88-95` vs `ORIG:60-75`).
- **[DEVIATION] D7 — radiating/burning suppressant substitution.** Original radiating decays 40x faster next to `sand_boron_layer` or boron `sand_mix` (`ORIG:RBMKDebrisRadiating.java:45-48`); port recognizes only the full `SAND_BORON` block (`PORT:RBMKDebrisRadiatingBlock.java:72`, `PORT:RBMKDebrisBurningBlock.isSuppressant:84-86`).
- **[DEVIATION] D8 — digamma debris gained radiation and slowed down.** Original: no radiation at all; spread = `onBlockAdded` schedules one update at **2 ticks**, each conversion re-triggers the chain (`ORIG:RBMKDebrisDigamma.java:15-33`). Port: applies `applyDigammaData(200)` over 32 blocks and reschedules itself every 20-40 ticks (`PORT:RBMKDebrisDigammaBlock.java:29-30,36-38,69-74`) — 10-20x slower spread, plus port-only digamma dosing. Conversion targets match (other RBMKDebris subclasses + both corium blocks; `PORT:62-67` vs `ORIG:23-30`).
- **[DEVIATION] D9 — debris block hardness/resistance.** Original pribris family: hardness **50**, resistance **600** (`ORIG:blocks/ModBlocks.java:2146-2149`). Port: plain `rbmk_debris` = gravel copy, `strength(0.5)` i.e. hardness 0.5 / resistance 0.5; variants `strength(50, 360)` (`PORT:block/ModBlocks.java:2384-2387`). - **[GLUE] G1** — original used scheduled block updates (100-120 / 20-40 tick cadences); port uses `onPlace` scheduleTick + `randomTicks()` flag with the same cadences (`PORT:RBMKDebrisBurningBlock.java:37-39,48`, `PORT:RBMKDebrisRadiatingBlock.java:57-65`). Flame via `RBMK_FLAME` particle vs `AuxParticlePacketNT("rbmkflame")`. `gas_meltdown` spread (1-in-10 into air) and fire ambient sound match.
